Introduction

The purpose of this project is to determine which factors are most strongly associated with student academic success. Student success will be measured using GPA. By analyzing variables such as study time, absences, parental support, tutoring, and extracurricular activities, I will identify which factors contribute most to academic performance.

Conclusion

This project examined factors associated with student academic success. GPA was used as the measure of academic performance. Exploratory data analysis, correlation analysis, and regression modeling were used to investigate relationships between student characteristics and GPA.

The results indicate that study time, absences, parental support, and other educational factors contribute to academic performance. The multiple regression model provided a stronger explanation of GPA than any single variable alone, suggesting that student success is influenced by multiple factors working together.
